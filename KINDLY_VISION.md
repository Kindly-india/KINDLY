# KINDLY_VISION.md

The product north star. Read before making any design or feature decision.

---

## The One-Sentence Version

**KINDLY is a social club where the entry ticket is doing something good.**

Volunteering is the vehicle. Community is the destination.

---

## Who We Are Building For

Riya / Arjun. 19–26. College student or first job. Lives in a tier-2 Indian city (Nashik first, then expanding). Spends 4+ hours a day on Instagram. Feels vaguely guilty about not "doing something meaningful." Genuinely wants connection but is exhausted by dating apps. Wants their life to look interesting. Has every intention of being a better person but has zero activation energy on Sunday morning.

We are not building for the 35-year-old NGO veteran. We are not building for the social worker. We are building for the 21-year-old who wants a reason to get off their phone on Sunday morning that doesn't feel like a chore.

---

## Why People Actually Show Up

The stated reason people show up is never the real reason people show up.

- Run clubs are officially about running. Actually about dating and belonging.
- Gyms are officially about fitness. Actually about identity and social proof.
- Volunteering is officially about doing good. Actually about meeting interesting people, having stories to tell, and feeling like the kind of person who shows up.

KINDLY does not pretend the second list doesn't exist. We design for it.

---

## The Three Layers Every Feature Should Serve

1. **First Feeling** — does opening KINDLY make someone feel something within 30 seconds? (Identity. Belonging. FOMO. Curiosity.)
2. **Reasons to Come Back** — is there something happening between events that pulls someone back into the app? (Squads. Weekly rhythm. Photos from last weekend. A friend just registered.)
3. **Identity Attachment** — does someone want to be seen as a KINDLY person? Would they share their profile the way they share their Instagram?

Every PR should be able to answer: which layer does this serve, and how?

---

## The Decision Filter

When we are tempted to build something, ask:

> Does this make showing up feel cool, social, and worth coming back to?

If yes, build it. If it only makes the platform more "useful" or "feature-complete" without making the experience more emotionally compelling, deprioritize it.

The volunteering must remain real. The impact must remain real. We are not gamifying charity into meaninglessness. But we are honest that the community is the product.

---

## The Two Things to Never Forget

1. **The platform is the scaffolding. The founder is the community.** For the first year, no feature substitutes for showing up to events in person and being magnetic.
2. **The After is as important as the Event.** Every event has two parts: The Cause (the volunteering) and The Connect (chai, breakfast, walk, bonfire afterwards). The Cause is the justification. The Connect is the motivation. The platform must treat both as first-class.
